AI-Powered Job Market & Skill Intelligence Analytics
Project Overview

The AI-Powered Job Market & Skill Intelligence Analytics project is an interactive, multi-page Power BI report designed to analyze job application demand, candidate skills, AI match scores, salary patterns, recruitment outcomes, and employment trends. It helps users explore how job roles, industries, cities, education, experience, and skill categories relate to application activity and recruitment outcomes.
The report contains six dashboard pages, including an executive insights page, with slicers that allow users to filter the analysis and compare results across relevant categories.

Problem Statement

The job market includes multiple roles, industries, skill categories, locations, and candidate profiles. Understanding these factors together can help identify areas of high application demand, compare AI match and skill match scores, examine salary patterns, and review recruitment outcomes.
This dashboard brings these measures together in one Power BI report. It supports exploration of:
Application demand by industry, job role, skill, and city.
Candidate application statuses, offers, joins, and conversion measures.
Average AI match score and average skill match.
Salary patterns by role, skill match, experience, education, and industry.
Skill-category demand and role-skill relationships.
Monthly and year-over-year application trends.
Executive-level findings that summarize key patterns in the report.

Recruitment outcomes: The report includes application-status, offer, join, and conversion visuals to compare recruitment outcomes across job roles.
Location patterns: Bengaluru has the highest candidate count among the cities shown, at approximately 4.4K, followed by Hyderabad at approximately 3.3K.
Skill categories: Cloud and Software are among the largest categories shown in the Skill Category Mix visual.
Trend analysis: The report includes monthly applications and year-over-year comparisons for reviewing changes over time.

Steps Followed

The following describes the report workflow at a high level, based on the supplied Power BI report:
Open the job market dataset in Power BI Desktop.
Review the available fields used for date, job role, skill, industry, city, education, experience, application status, AI match, skill match, and salary analysis.
Prepare the data for reporting and ensure that fields used for comparisons have suitable data types.
Create or verify relationships between relevant tables, if the source dataset uses multiple tables.
Create the required measures for application counts, unique candidates, average AI match score, average skill match, average salary, and recruitment/trend indicators.
Build the report pages and add visuals for job demand, recruitment outcomes, skills, salary, geography, and trends.
Add slicers so users can filter the report by the available dimensions.
Format page titles, KPI cards, chart labels, navigation, and page layouts consistently.
Validate displayed results against the source data and check how filters affect the measures.
Publish the report to Power BI Service if online sharing is required.

How to Use the Dashboard

Open the report in Power BI Desktop or Power BI Service, depending on access.
Use the slicers at the top of each page to filter by the available dimensions.
Compare KPI cards to understand the overall application, candidate, match-score, and salary picture.
Explore job-role and industry visuals to compare demand and recruitment outcomes.
Use the skill and experience visuals to examine skill categories and AI/skill match patterns.
Review the trend visuals to compare application activity over time.
Finish on the Executive Insights page for the report's summarized findings.

Conclusion

The AI-Powered Job Market & Skill Intelligence Analytics report brings job demand, skills, AI match indicators, salary patterns, recruitment outcomes, and time trends into a single interactive Power BI solution. It enables users to explore the data from multiple perspectives and use the report's findings to guide further investigation of hiring demand and skill alignment.
